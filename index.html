All LATAM media in one place

Prisma Noticias is a digital news kiosk and intelligent news aggregator. It provides access to a carefully curated inventory of media and news from Latam and the world, with local, national and global coverage. It also makes it possible to see how a wide range of outlets cover relevant events in the region.

Features

All the news and its different perspectives in a single read

01

Access to all media from a single place

  • Integrated press hub.Centralizes 940+ outlets (local, provincial, national and international) in a single direct access point.
  • Digital kiosk by countries and regions.Explore and open the digital editions of each newspaper with a single tap, without having to remember URLs, open dozens of tabs or browse cluttered browsers.
  • Assisted geographic setup.Interactive map to discover and prioritize outlets from your province or region, along with global reference mastheads (The Economist, Bloomberg Línea, El País, BBC, etc.).
02

Multi-perspective view of the editorial spectrum

  • Editorial line contrast.Each event brings together the different views of the informative spectrum (social, institutional, economic and geopolitical), letting the reader see how each newspaper frames, ranks and headlines the same reality.
  • Diversity without a single bias.Breaks filter bubbles by placing outlets with different editorial tendencies side by side on the same news event.
  • Source transparency.Clear identification of the outlet of origin, its provenance and coverage to foster the reader's own judgment.
03

Smart grouping of news by event

  • One card per event (Clusters).Consolidates dozens of redundant articles into a single organized news block, eliminating duplication in the feed.
  • Neutral, bilingual headline.AI-synthesized representative headline in the active language (English or Spanish), stripped of sensationalist clickbait.
  • Comparative source carousel.Within each event, a dynamic carousel gathers every outlet covering the story in real time to jump from one point of view to another instantly.
04

Fast and optimized reading experience

  • High-speed WebView reader.Access to each outlet's original site with early load cutoff, avoiding the wait for heavy ads and invasive trackers.
  • Smooth offline reading (local cache).Smart local storage lets you keep reading news and front pages on public transport or areas with intermittent signal.
  • Total anonymous privacy.No accounts, emails or mandatory sign-ups required; all preferences and settings stay exclusively on the user's device.
05

Consumption modes for every moment

  • Curated News.The panoramic summary with the day's major events and clusters.
  • Live feed.Minute-by-minute chronological flow with instant filtering by newspaper, country or category.
  • "Variety" section.Independent space for culture, technology, entertainment and gaming, keeping hard news free of miscellany.
  • Specialized Sports filter.Coverage organized by specific sports disciplines (soccer, tennis, basketball, motorsport, etc.).
